APPENDIX  ·  How We Did This

The methodology behind the report, in plain English.

Where the data came from

Live Google AI Overview answers and Google search data, captured via premium research tools. All numbers are US-based, captured in early June 2026.

How we ran the test

We fed Google's AI category questions covering computer vision, YOLO, object detection, image annotation, and AI vision platforms. We recorded every Overview answer and checked whether Roboflow was named.

We then verified the most important per-keyword claims against the live Google SERP. For four high-volume queries (including "what is Roboflow" and "image labeling tool") we pulled the actual AI Overview that Google returns right now and inspected its source list and inline links for roboflow.com. This step is mandatory in every audit since the Fetcher.ai run shipped two false "ranks but not in the Overview" claims that better verification caught.

Why some keywords show "Not tested"

Roboflow ranks for 3,044 keywords. We verified four against live SERPs in this audit. Several more likely already earn citations and just haven't been individually checked. Running them through live SERP checks is Move 03 in the action plan.

What's in the AI Visibility Score

A weighted average of five things Google's AI rewards: how the AI describes the brand, whether it's included in category answers, whether its own pages get quoted, how broadly it shows up across the category, and whether branded searches return clean results. 0 means absent. 100 means dominant.

Why the brand sits at 58 not higher

The brand-name AI Overview is exceptional (80/100 on identity). The site is cited in two real category Overviews (50/100 on inclusion). The remaining 90 days of work expand category coverage. With three additional verified category Overviews, the score moves to ~68 to 72. The ceiling is "Dominant band," reachable but requires sustained content shape work.

What this audit does not do

It does not benchmark share-of-mentions against a chosen list of competitors (Ultralytics is the natural rival), and it does not connect to live Google Search Console data. Both are useful next steps.
