EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

APPENDIX  ·  How We Did This

The methodology behind the report, in plain English.

Where the data came from

Live Google AI Overview answers and Google search data, captured via premium research tools. All numbers are US-based, captured in late May and early June 2026.

How we ran the test

We fed Google's AI category questions covering customer success software, customer health, churn, conversation intelligence, account risk, and AI context layers. We recorded every AI Overview answer and counted whether Sturdy was named. We also read the cited pages by hand to identify false matches.

Why the raw number is misleading

A tracking tool reported 40,710 "Sturdy" mentions in this market. We inspected the top 25 source domains. They are Home Depot, Lowe's, Etsy, Wikihow, The Spruce, Serious Eats. The tool is counting every use of "sturdy" as a word, not as a brand. So we set the real brand-citation count to zero and use that throughout the report.

What's in the AI Visibility Score

A weighted average of five things Google's AI rewards: how the AI describes the brand, whether it's named in category answers, whether its own pages get quoted, how broadly it shows up across category topics, and whether branded searches return clean results. 0 means absent. 100 means dominant.

What this audit does not do

This is a brand-only positioning diagnostic. It does not benchmark share-of-mentions against a chosen list of competitors, and it does not connect to live Google Search Console data. Both are useful next steps.

A note on the brand name

"Sturdy" is a high-frequency English adjective. This makes the brand harder to discover, harder to measure, and harder to differentiate in AI Overviews. A consistent "Sturdy AI" anchor in all content is a structural fix that compounds over time.
