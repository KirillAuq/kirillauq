JS Conditions (Pro+) ABM logic is rarely simple. Multi-signal conditions like "Tier 1 account + LinkedIn campaign + pricing page visit" can't be expressed in dropdown menus Developers implement precise multi-signal audience logic without predefined menu constraints
Full-Stack / Server-Side Testing (Pro+) ABM needs to go beyond copy swaps. Teams need to test pricing pages, gated content logic, and API-driven recommendations for specific accounts Product, engineering, and marketing teams collaborate on deep personalization, not just headline tests

Frequentist + Bayesian Statistics ABM teams and their data counterparts often trust different statistical frameworks. Forcing one model creates friction and misaligned conclusions Marketing, data analysts, and C-suite stakeholders all interpret results in the statistical language they trust
