Executive Summary

My Marketing Pro is missing from Google's AI Overviews. Both competitors are not.

When you ask Google a marketing question and the AI answer box appears at the top, it does not name My Marketing Pro. It does name Data Axle and LocaliQ.

One number looks misleading at first. A tracking tool reports that the brand has 28% of the mentions in this category. That number is wrong. The phrase "my marketing pro" is everyday English. The tool is counting those words, not real mentions of the company. After we filter that out by hand, real mentions drop to zero.

The cause is not strategy. It is foundation. The site has a low authority score (37 vs. 77 and 83). It ranks for zero keywords in Google's US search results. Google's AI almost always quotes pages that already rank well. Until those pages exist, the brand cannot be quoted.

Generative Engine Optimization. The practice of getting your brand named or quoted by AI answers (Google AI, ChatGPT, Perplexity).

07 · What To Do in the Next 30 Days

Five moves, in order of impact, each tied to a finding above.

Move 01 · On the site

Build the right pages.

Publish pages that directly answer the questions buyers ask Google. Examples: "Best AI marketing platform for small business," "AI marketing agents that book meetings," "All-in-one marketing platform with data included," "Pay-as-you-go marketing platform." Each page should start with a one-paragraph plain-English definition, include a comparison table, and end with an FAQ.

Without these pages, nothing else compounds. Google's AI almost always quotes pages that already rank in regular search.

Move 02 · On the site

Get the customer stories out of pop-ups.

The vodka launch, the HVAC 500% lower customer cost, and the Times Square Nasdaq tower stories are great proof. They all live inside click-to-expand pop-ups today, which Google's AI cannot read. Publish each as a regular page with clear headings and visible numbers. Then Google can quote them.

Move 03 · Outside the site

Get on the "best of" lists.

The top six "best marketing platform" and "best SEO agency" lists Google's AI quotes most are firstpagesage.com, thriveagency.com, clutch.co, onelittleweb.com, outpaceseo.com, and silverbackstrategies.com. Pitch each one to add My Marketing Pro, with a clear differentiator and one verifiable customer result. One placement on each can shift Google's AI answers within 60 days.

Move 04 · Outside the site

Set up listings on review sites.

Build active vendor listings on g2.com, capterra.com, softwareadvice.com, trustradius.com, and gartner.com. Use the platform to nudge existing customers (starting with the free Build & Launch users, the lowest friction group) to leave reviews. These are the canonical sources Google's AI uses for "is this vendor any good" questions.

Move 05 · Positioning

Pick one topic to own this quarter.

Three big topics have no clear winner: "AI agents that actually do the work," "Connected TV and digital billboards for SMBs," and "brand ads and direct response unified." We recommend leading with "AI agents that actually do the work" first. The brand's product already delivers it. No competitor owns it in Google's AI yet. And the proof is easy to show: 4-minute launches, AI voice calls, the sequence generator. Trying to be the answer for all 11 channels and three plans at once is spreading too thin.

Appendix · How We Did This

The methodology behind the report, in plain English.

Where the data came from

We pulled live Google AI Overview answers and search data using premium research tools. All numbers are US-based, captured in late May and early June 2026.

How we ran the test

We tracked three brands: My Marketing Pro, Data Axle, and LocaliQ. We fed Google's AI a standard set of category and comparison questions, recorded every answer, and counted who got mentioned. We also read every answer by hand to filter out false matches.

Why the "real" share of mentions is different

The tracking tool counted 25 mentions for My Marketing Pro. We read all of them. None were actual mentions of the brand. They were sentences like "track my marketing pro forma" that just happen to contain the phrase. So we set real mentions to zero and recalculated the percentages between the two real competitors.

What the limits are

You chose the competitors. They are valid business competitors, but Google's AI does not see them as direct comparison points to My Marketing Pro right now (the three brands never share an answer). The picture here is more about positioning than head-to-head.

A note about the brand name

"My Marketing Pro" is also a common English phrase. That makes it harder for tracking tools and AI systems to tell the brand apart from regular sentences. Either a more distinctive name, or always using "MMP" as the trademarked short form, would make progress easier to measure.

What this report doesn't cover

It doesn't compare you to the brands you call competitors on your own homepage (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Salesforce Marketing Cloud). It also doesn't connect to your live Google Search Console data. Both are useful next steps.
