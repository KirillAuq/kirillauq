GEO
Generative Engine Optimization. The practice of making a brand's content easy for AI systems to find, understand, and quote in their answers.
MCP (Model Context Protocol)
An open protocol letting AI agents connect to external tools and data sources. Willow's platform manages which MCP connections enterprise employees and agents can use, and under what rules.

APPENDIX  ·  Methodology and Data Notes

How this audit was built and what the data means.

Sources

Google AI Overviews data: Ahrefs Brand Radar, default prompt database, US country setting. All brand data uses URL-group matching (withwillow.ai, subdomains scope) to remove name-pollution false positives. Organic search metrics: Ahrefs Site Explorer, US, June 2026.

Competitor derivation

Ahrefs Site Explorer organic competitors returned zero results for withwillow.ai (no ranking keywords yet means no keyword overlap). Competitors were taken from Willow's own published compare pages: Archestra, Cloudflare, MetaMCP, MintMCP, Obot, Runlayer. Cloudflare was excluded from the final set because its brand mentions in AIO are overwhelmingly about CDN and DDoS, not AI Gateway, which would pollute the share-of-mentions comparison.

Why all brands show zero

Ahrefs Brand Radar operates on a database of prompts reflecting commonly-searched queries. The AI agent governance category has not yet generated enough search query volume to appear in this database. This is not a measurement error. It confirms the category is in its pre-AIO phase. The race has not started. This is the most important finding in the report.

What this audit does not cover

This audit covers Google AI Overviews only. It does not cover ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or other AI answer systems. It does not include Google Search Console clickstream data. Adding those inputs would unlock a more complete picture and is recommended for future audit cycles once the brand has established some organic presence.
