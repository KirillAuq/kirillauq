Move 03  ·  Outside the site

Push customer reviews on G2 and Capterra.

Fetcher already has presence on G2, Capterra, GetApp, SourceForge. Volume is what's missing. Run an in-product nudge to active accounts (Magnite, Counterpart, Temedica, Bevi, Docker as starters) to leave verified reviews. Target 50+ G2 reviews in 60 days to compete with Recruiterflow.

Move 04  ·  On the site

Publish the "Inbound Recruiting" canonical answer.

Fetcher just launched an Inbound Recruiting product. Google's AI has no canonical "what is inbound recruiting" answer yet. Write it first. Definition page with diagram, comparison vs. traditional outbound sourcing, FAQ. This is the rare moment when Fetcher can claim a category before competitors do.

Move 05  ·  Brand

Always pair "Fetcher" with a qualifier.

"Fetcher" by itself is a retriever dog, a rugby flanker, a YA novel character, and an Elder Scrolls insult. The Google AI Overview for the bare word is a dictionary entry. Always write "Fetcher AI" or "Fetcher.ai" in published content (press, social, ads, blog metadata, customer stories). The single move that makes everything else compound faster.

APPENDIX  ·  How We Did This

The methodology behind the report, in plain English.

Where the data came from

Live Google AI Overview answers and Google search data, captured via premium research tools. All numbers are US-based, captured in early June 2026.

How we ran the test

We fed Google's AI category questions covering AI recruiting tools, candidate sourcing software, ATS systems, recruitment CRMs, candidate engagement, and passive sourcing. We recorded every Overview answer and checked whether Fetcher was named.

We then verified the most important per-keyword claims against the live Google SERP. For four high-volume category keywords we pulled the actual AI Overview that Google returns right now and inspected its source list and inline links for fetcher.ai. This step caught two false "ranks but not in the Overview" claims that the sampled tracking data would have shipped — and it's why this report's score is higher than a first-pass read would suggest.

Why the brand is in a different position than the last few audits

Unlike Sturdy or My Marketing Pro, Fetcher already has a correct brand-name AI Overview AND two verified category Overview citations. The brand has crossed from "described correctly when asked by name" to "named in answers for at least some category questions." The work now is breadth, not foundation.

What's in the AI Visibility Score

A weighted average of five things Google's AI rewards: how the AI describes the brand, whether it's included in category answers, whether its own pages get quoted, how broadly it shows up across the category, and whether branded searches return clean results. 0 means absent. 100 means dominant.

A note on the brand name

"Fetcher" matches several other things: a retriever dog, a rugby term, an Elder Scrolls insult, a Daughter of Smoke and Bone plot device, "Fetcher Express" courier, and "American Fetcher" hats. Pairing the name with "AI" in all content removes most of this ambiguity.

What this audit does not do

It does not benchmark share-of-mentions against a chosen list of competitors, and it does not connect to live Google Search Console data. Both are useful next steps if you want to track progress quarter over quarter.
